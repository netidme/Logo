Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Core Commitment: All core features of this tool run locally in your browser. Images you upload, text you enter, and logos you generate are never uploaded to our servers.

1. What Information We Collect

1.1 Local Processing, No Uploads

All logo creation features of this tool are completed within your browser. Images you upload, text you enter, and colors you select are processed locally only and are not sent to any server.

1.2 Information We Do Not Actively Collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or address
  • We do not require account registration
  • We do not record the content of the logos you create

1.3 Information That May Be Passively Collected

When you visit this website, the server may automatically record the following anonymous information for statistics and service optimization:

  • Visit time, pages visited
  • Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • IP address (used for statistics only, not linked to personal identity)
  • Referrer URL

2. How Information Is Used

The anonymous information we collect is used solely for:

  • Analyzing user behavior to optimize the product experience
  • Troubleshooting technical issues to ensure service stability
  • Preventing malicious attacks and abuse

We will not use the information for advertising, user profiling, or any commercial purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This tool uses the following third-party services, which may have their own privacy policies:

  • Iconify API — Used to load IconPark icons
  • jsDelivr CDN — Used to load online fonts
  • cdnjs — Used to load the JSZip library

These services only provide static resources and do not collect your personal information.

4. Cookie Usage

This tool itself does not use cookies. However, third-party services (such as CDNs) may use cookies to cache resources. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

5. Data Security

Since all core features of this tool run locally and do not involve data uploads, there is no risk of data leakage. We also take necessary measures to prevent the website from being maliciously tampered with.

6. Protection of Minors

This tool is intended for users of all ages. We do not actively collect any personal information from minors. If a guardian discovers that a minor has provided information to us, they may contact us for deletion.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your personally identifiable information, there are no data access, modification, or deletion operations involved. If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to contact us.

8. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time, and any updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of this tool constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
