Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the tool free?

A: Yes, all features are completely free and require no registration. Generated logos can be downloaded and used for free.

Q: Can I use the generated logo commercially?

A: Yes, it can be used for commercial projects. However, we recommend verifying the specific licenses of the icons and fonts you use, as different resources may have slightly different terms.

Q: Why do some fonts load slowly?

A: Chinese font files are usually quite large. We use on-demand loading, meaning a font only starts downloading when you select it. The first use of a font may take a few seconds, after which it will be cached.

Q: Can I upload my own icon?

A: Yes. In the "Icon Content" panel, you can upload a custom PNG/JPG image to use as the logo icon.

Q: What resolution are the exported images?

A: Single download defaults to a 1024×1024 HD PNG. Multi-size batch download outputs 8 sizes: 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024.
