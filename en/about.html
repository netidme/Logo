About Us

Welcome to the Free Logo Generator! We are a team dedicated to providing free online design tools, hoping to help everyone in need quickly create their own logo through simple and easy-to-use products.

Our Motivation

On the journey of starting a business, opening a shop, running a self-media account, or organizing an event, many people encounter a common problem: they need a logo, but don't want to pay a high price for a designer, nor do they know how to use professional software.

Traditional solutions either cost money or require a lot of time learning professional tools like Photoshop and Illustrator. We believe that making a logo shouldn't be this hard.

So we built this tool, allowing everyone to create a professional-looking logo just by opening their browser.

What We Offer

🎨 Completely Free

No registration, ready to use. All features are open for free, with no paywall.

🖼 Rich Icons

Built-in ByteDance IconPark library, covering 2600+ icons across various industries.

🔤 Diverse Fonts

Integrates 8 free fonts including Smiley Sans, MiSans, Source Han Sans, covering business, artistic, and personal styles.

🎯 Smart Layout

8 common logo layout templates, one-click apply, no design experience required.

📦 HD Export

Supports single PNG download, multi-size batch export, transparent background, meeting different scenarios.

✨ Free for Commercial Use

Generated logos can be used in commercial projects (please verify the specific licenses of fonts and icons).

Our Philosophy

We believe a good tool should be:

  • Simple — No manual needed, ready to use out of the box
  • Fast — From idea to finished product in under 5 minutes
  • Free — Affordable for everyone
  • Transparent — Clear and traceable sources for icons and fonts

About Copyright

The icons used in this tool are from IconPark (ByteDance open-source project), and the fonts are from various open-source projects (such as MiSans, Source Han Sans, etc.), all following their respective open-source licenses.

When generating a logo, users can freely combine icons, fonts, colors, and text. Since the licensing terms of different icons and fonts may vary, we recommend verifying whether they fit your use case before official use.

This tool only provides creation functionality and makes no guarantee regarding the final copyright ownership of the logo.
